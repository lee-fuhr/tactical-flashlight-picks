Best for specific needs

When a light might be used under extreme duress, reliability and intuitive controls matter more than raw specs. The SureFire EDCL2-T’s two-stage tailcap lets you instantly select a low or high beam without cycling through modes, and its proven track record with law enforcement makes it the gold standard. For a slightly more affordable option that still excels in a defensive role, the Streamlight ProTac HL 5-X USB provides a similar no-nonsense interface with the added convenience of USB-C charging. Our pick: SureFire EDCL2-T.

Best for Outdoor Exploration and Search

If you’re hiking, camping, or involved in search and rescue, you need a light that can both light up a distant trail sign and provide enough spill to see your immediate surroundings. The Fenix TK20R UE is the top choice here thanks to its balanced beam and sturdy build. For those who prioritize maximum throw to spot landmarks or wildlife far away, the NITECORE P30i offers exceptional distance for a very low price. Our pick: Fenix TK20R UE.

Best for Extreme Brightness

Sometimes you need to turn night into day across a wide area. The Olight Seeker 4 Pro is built for exactly that, with its high-output flood beam that fills a room or a clearing effortlessly. It’s not a thrower, so don’t expect long-distance pinpoint reach, but for lighting up a whole scene – whether for security sweeps or setting up camp – it’s unmatched in this lineup. Our pick: Olight Seeker 4 Pro.

Our verdict

For most people, the Streamlight ProTac HL 5-X USB is the smartest choice because it delivers nearly everything you could want in a tactical flashlight – ample brightness, a versatile beam, USB-C charging, and a rugged build – without straying into extreme pricing. And if you need a tank that will never let you down in the worst conditions, save up for the SureFire EDCL2-T. Whichever you choose, you’ll have a tool that earns its place in your gear.
